Hello! I'm Ma Yilin.
I graduated from Hainan University with a bachelor’s degree in English, and I will pursue my master’s degree in Computational Communication.
My main interest is international communication. I am concerned with breaking stereotypes, bridging cultural gaps and facilitating sincere cross-cultural exchanges.
I view computational communication as applying computational social science methods to communication research, to make arguments more sufficient and rigorous.
I have practical experience in English teaching, media operation and translation.
